Growth Hormone: Your Body's Night Builder

Your body makes a helper called growth hormone. It works hardest while you sleep. Here is what it does and how to help it.

How it works

It is like a text message from your brain to your body.

1

Your brain

A tiny spot the size of a pea makes the hormone.

2

A message goes out

It travels in your blood to the whole body.

3

Your body gets to work

Muscles, bones, and fat all get the message.

What it does for you

Grow tall

Helps kids grow.

Strong muscles

Helps keep muscle.

Strong bones

Keeps bones solid.

Burn fat

Turns stored fat into energy.

Help your body make more

Your everyday habits make a big difference.

These help

Good sleep. Go to bed at the same time each night.
Move hard. Lift weights or walk fast up a hill.
Close the kitchen early. Stop eating a few hours before bed.
Lose belly fat. Your body makes more as belly fat goes down.

These lower it

Short sleep. Staying up late means less of it.
Late-night sweets. Sugar before bed gets in the way.
Alcohol at night. A drink before bed lowers it.
Extra belly fat. It makes your body send out less.

What about shots and peptides?

Some people take shots or pills to get more. Think of a traffic light.

Green light

Approved by the FDA. Only for people a doctor has tested and found low.

Growth hormone shots

Yellow light

Made by special pharmacies. Not well tested for most people. Ask your doctor.

Red light

Sold online as "research chemicals." Nobody checks what is really inside.
