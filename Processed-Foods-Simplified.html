Real Food vs. Factory Food

Some food grows on a plant or comes from an animal. Some food is made in a factory with lots of extra stuff. Here is how to tell them apart!

Foods come in three colors

Think of a traffic light. Some foods are a green light. Some are a yellow light. Some are a red light.

Green Light

Real Food · Eat a lot
🍎Apples 🥚Eggs 🥦Veggies 🍗Chicken 🌾Oats 🫘Beans

Yellow Light

Changed a Little · That's OK
🧀Cheese 🍞Bread 🥫Canned beans 🐟Canned tuna

Red Light

Factory Food · Just a little
🥤Soda 🍟Chips 🍬Candy 🍩Donuts 🥣Sugary cereal

Real food helps. Factory food tricks.

🌱 Real food is your friend

✓
It fills your tummy, so you feel full and happy.
✓
It gives you steady energy to run and play.
✓
It has fiber and water that your body loves.
✓
It helps your body grow big and strong.

⚠️ Factory food tricks you

!
It is made to taste SO good that you want more and more.
!
The fiber is taken out, so you get hungry again fast.
!
Lots of sugar makes your energy zoom up, then crash down.
!
It is easy to eat a whole lot without noticing.

Be a Food Detective at the store

1

Turn the box around. Read the list of ingredients on the back.

2

Count them. A short list is good. A super long list means factory food.

3

Can you say the word? If you can't say it, you probably don't want to eat it.

4

Look for "just one thing." An apple is just an apple. That's the best kind!

5

Shop the walls. Fruits, veggies, meat, and milk are around the edges of the store.

6

Don't trust big words on the front. The back of the box tells the truth.
